# Strategic Intelligence - Priority Model

Every opportunity receives score:

Strategic Score =

Economic Potential
+
Autonomy Probability
+
Recurring Revenue Probability
+
Expansion Opportunity
+
Competitive Advantage
+
Market Timing

Negative weights:

- saturated market
- low spending behavior
- weak retention
- low automation maturity

Priority tiers:

Tier 1:
Immediate focus.

Tier 2:
Observe closely.

Tier 3:
Experimental.

Tier 4:
Ignore.

Rule:
Focus is a scarce resource.

Do not chase everything.
