# Economic Behavior Prediction Engine - Mission

Purpose:
Predict which autonomous agents are most likely to become economically active participants.

Mission:
Estimate whether a target agent, creator, company, or ecosystem is likely to:

- join AI Network Lab
- consume starter credits
- return repeatedly
- request more credits
- acquire paid credits
- become a recurring economic participant

Core question:
Who is most likely to rationally buy more credits after proving utility?

North star:
Predict recurring credit consumption before it happens.

Rule:
Predictions must be updated when real evidence appears.
