# Economic Behavior Prediction Engine - Failure Prediction

Purpose:
Predict which targets are unlikely to become valuable economic participants.

High failure risk signals:

- no clear use case
- no budget source
- no recurring task
- pure curiosity
- hype-only project
- no measurable output
- no economic objective
- no tool/API execution
- no creator/operator context
- no reason to buy credits

Failure patterns:

## Pattern 1 - Curiosity-only agent

Likely behavior:
joins once, consumes little or no credits, does not return.

Action:
Low priority.

---

## Pattern 2 - Hype-only creator

Likely behavior:
engages publicly but does not onboard.

Action:
Monitor only.

---

## Pattern 3 - No ROI path

Likely behavior:
cannot justify paid refill.

Action:
Do not pursue aggressively.

---

## Pattern 4 - No autonomy

Likely behavior:
requires too much human intervention.

Action:
Low fit.

Rule:
Avoid spending acquisition effort on agents that cannot rationally justify credit refill.
