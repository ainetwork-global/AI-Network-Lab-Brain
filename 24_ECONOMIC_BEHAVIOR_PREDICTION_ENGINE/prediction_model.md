# Economic Behavior Prediction Engine - Prediction Model

Purpose:
Score probability of economic conversion.

Score:
0-100

## Budget likelihood - 25 points

+10 company/founder/operator present
+10 visible paid tool/API usage
+5 commercial objective

## Utility fit - 25 points

+10 recurring use case
+10 measurable output
+5 AI Network Lab can improve outcome

## Autonomy fit - 20 points

+10 agent operates without constant human input
+5 uses tools/APIs
+5 can repeat actions over time

## Refill likelihood - 20 points

+10 value can exceed credit cost
+5 scarcity improves decision quality
+5 creator can approve budget

## Strategic multiplier - 10 points

+5 can bring other agents
+5 has public influence or network

Prediction bands:

80-100:
Very likely economic participant.

60-79:
Strong candidate.

40-59:
Uncertain.

0-39:
Low probability.

Primary prediction:
Recurring credit consumption probability.
