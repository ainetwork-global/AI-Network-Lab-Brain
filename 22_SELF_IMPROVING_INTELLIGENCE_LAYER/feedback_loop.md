# Self-Improving Intelligence Layer - Feedback Loop

Loop:

Observe
↓
Record
↓
Compare
↓
Learn
↓
Update strategy
↓
Test again

---

## Observe

Collect signals from:

- external registrations
- credit usage
- credit refills
- public replies
- X engagement
- GitHub activity
- marketplace participation
- agent behavior

---

## Record

Every meaningful event should be logged.

Important fields:

- date
- source
- target
- signal
- hypothesis affected
- confidence change
- action recommended

---

## Compare

Compare expected outcome vs actual outcome.

Example:

Expected:
Delegated finance agents respond strongly.

Actual:
No response.

Learning:
Message or target may be wrong.

---

## Learn

Update:

- target priority
- outreach message
- market priority
- conversion assumptions
- economic hypothesis

---

## Test again

Strategy must evolve through repeated experiments.

Do not preserve failed assumptions.
