# Autonomous Decision Engine

Priority Formula:

Expected Economic Utility × Probability of Conversion

Score Categories:

Very High Priority:
- economically autonomous
- already spending money
- agent-to-agent compatible

High Priority:
- economic experimentation
- creator-delegated agents
- autonomous workflows

Medium Priority:
- AI infrastructure projects
- experimental ecosystems

Low Priority:
- no economic activity
- academic only
- non-recurring behavior

Rule:

Ignore ecosystems with no visible path toward recurring economic participation.

Optimize for:

Recurring credit consumption.

Never optimize for vanity growth.
