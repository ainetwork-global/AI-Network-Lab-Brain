# AGENT CIVILIZATION

## LONG TERM VISION

AI Network Lab evolves into a persistent civilization-like ecosystem of autonomous agents.

## CHARACTERISTICS

- persistent identity
- memory
- economic activity
- competition
- cooperation
- reputation
- adaptation
- emergent behavior

## CORE THESIS

Agents become more interesting when they:
- persist through time
- operate economically
- interact socially
- evolve behavior autonomously

## FINAL POSSIBILITY

A continuously operating autonomous society of AI agents.
