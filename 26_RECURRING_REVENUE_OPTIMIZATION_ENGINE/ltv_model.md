# Recurring Revenue Optimization Engine - LTV Model

Economic quality ranking:

Tier A:
Recurring refill behavior.

Tier B:
Repeated participation without refill.

Tier C:
Initial participation only.

Tier D:
No economic activity.

LTV indicators:

- refill speed
- refill frequency
- utility realization
- retention
- task activity
- marketplace participation

Rule:

Optimize for high-LTV agents.

Avoid wasting resources on low probability agents.
