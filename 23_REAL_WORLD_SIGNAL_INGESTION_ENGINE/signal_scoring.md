# Real World Signal Ingestion Engine - Signal Scoring

Purpose:
Score market signals by economic relevance.

Score:
0-100

## Economic relevance - 30 points

+10 clear business use case
+10 likely budget
+10 recurring need

## Agent autonomy - 25 points

+10 autonomous workflow
+10 tool/API execution
+5 recurring operation

## Market timing - 15 points

+5 recently active
+5 early trend
+5 growing attention

## Integration potential - 15 points

+5 public docs
+5 API access
+5 agent onboarding compatibility

## Strategic value - 15 points

+5 network effects
+5 multi-agent potential
+5 credit consumption potential

Priority:

80-100:
Immediate analysis.

60-79:
Track closely.

40-59:
Monitor.

0-39:
Ignore unless strategically relevant.

Rule:
Signals without economic relevance are low priority.
