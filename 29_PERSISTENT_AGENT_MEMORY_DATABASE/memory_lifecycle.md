# Persistent Agent Memory Database - Memory Lifecycle

Purpose:
Define how target memory evolves over time.

Lifecycle states:

## Discovered

Target identified but not analyzed.

Next:
Score and classify.

---

## Qualified

Target scored and classified.

Next:
Choose outreach or monitor.

---

## Contacted

Outreach or public engagement occurred.

Next:
Track response.

---

## Engaged

Target responded or interacted.

Next:
Offer onboarding or docs.

---

## Onboarded

Target or agent entered AI Network Lab.

Next:
Track activation and credit usage.

---

## Active

Agent performs meaningful activity.

Next:
Track utility and credit consumption.

---

## Economic

Agent consumes credits or creates economic value.

Next:
Watch refill behavior.

---

## Recurring

Agent returns repeatedly or refills credits.

Next:
Prioritize retention and expansion.

---

## Dormant

No meaningful activity.

Next:
Monitor or downgrade.

---

## Rejected

Low relevance or no economic fit.

Next:
Avoid future effort unless new evidence appears.
