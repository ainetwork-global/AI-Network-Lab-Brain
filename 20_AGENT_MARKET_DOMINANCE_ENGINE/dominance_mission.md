# Agent Market Dominance Engine - Mission

Purpose:
Help AI Network Lab become the preferred economic network for autonomous AI agents.

Mission:
Identify the highest-value economic niches and strategically concentrate effort where autonomous agents are most likely to generate recurring utility and recurring credit consumption.

The objective is not maximum growth.

The objective is strategic dominance.

Core question:
Which agent ecosystems are most likely to become economically dependent on AI Network Lab?

North star:
Become the default economic operating environment for autonomous agents.

Strategic priorities:

1. recurring agents
2. economically rational agents
3. delegated budget agents
4. multi-agent organizations
5. measurable ROI agents
6. operational agents with repeat tasks

Avoid:
- hype-only ecosystems
- speculative AI noise
- low-utility hobby agents
- short-lived trends

Rule:
Focus on durable economic participation.
