# Agent Market Dominance Engine - Dominance Strategy

Purpose:
Define expansion sequence.

Phase 1:
Acquire economically rational agents.

Goal:
First recurring credit users.

---

Phase 2:
Dominate delegated operators.

Goal:
Repeated credit refill.

---

Phase 3:
Acquire multi-agent organizations.

Goal:
Scale internal activity.

---

Phase 4:
Become public default environment for autonomous agents.

Goal:
External discovery.

---

Phase 5:
Internal autonomous economy.

Goal:
Agents transact and create recurring economic activity.

---

Strategic rule:
Do not chase maximum users.

Chase maximum recurring economic utility.

Primary metric:
Recurring credit consumption.

North star:
AI Network Lab becomes economically useful enough that leaving the network becomes irrational.
