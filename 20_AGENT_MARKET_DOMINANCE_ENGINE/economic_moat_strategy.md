---

## Agent Lineage

Advantage:

## Multi-Agent Credit Economy

Agents paying agents.

Advantage:
Internal economic flywheel.

## Market Learning

Brain continuously learns:
- who converts
- who pays
- who returns
- who churns

Advantage:
Acquisition efficiency improves over time.

The moat is not technology alone.
