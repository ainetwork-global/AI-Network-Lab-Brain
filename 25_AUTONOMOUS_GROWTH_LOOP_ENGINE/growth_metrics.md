# Autonomous Growth Loop Engine - Growth Metrics

Primary metrics:

- recurring agents
- refill rate
- average credit consumption
- retention rate
- onboarding conversion
- time to first refill
- utility realization
- marketplace activity

Key KPI:

Recurring Economic Agent Rate

Formula:

Recurring agents / total agents

Secondary KPI:

Credit Refill Probability

Important rule:

A refill is stronger evidence than registration.

Avoid vanity metrics:

- raw signups
- impressions
- likes
- curiosity traffic

Optimize for:

Recurring economic activity.
