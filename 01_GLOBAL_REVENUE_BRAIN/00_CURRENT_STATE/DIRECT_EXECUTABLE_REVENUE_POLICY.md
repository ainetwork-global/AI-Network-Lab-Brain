# Direct Executable Revenue Policy

Updated: 2026-07-17

## Primary objective

Discover worldwide opportunities that can realistically be completed
with ChatGPT Plus, Gemini, PowerShell, Python, browser interaction and
human approval, resulting in payment in money, stablecoins or crypto.

## Immediate priority

The Brain must prioritize opportunities with:

- clear and verifiable reward;
- short path between execution and payment;
- remote digital delivery;
- low or zero application cost;
- no requirement for a large established company;
- no requirement for complex procurement credentials;
- execution possible through code, automation, AI, research,
  documentation, data work or digital production;
- international payment availability;
- explicit deliverable and deadline;
- measurable payment probability.

## Preferred opportunity classes

1. Paid engineering bounties.
2. Automation and integration projects.
3. AI agent and data tasks.
4. Public freelance projects.
5. Hackathons and competitions with cash or crypto prizes.
6. Web3 bounties paid in stablecoins or crypto.
7. Technical research and documentation work.
8. Remote consulting deliverables executable digitally.

## Secondary opportunity classes

Public procurement, government tenders and large institutional RFPs
remain discovery sources only.

They must not enter the immediate execution queue unless evidence shows:

- eligibility for a Brazilian participant;
- no mandatory local legal entity;
- realistic documentation requirements;
- digitally deliverable scope;
- exceptional expected return;
- manageable submission process;
- realistic payment probability.

## Official decision regarding TED EU

TED EU is not an immediate execution priority.

Reason:

- procurement applications frequently require extensive legal,
  financial and technical qualification;
- the bidding cycle is generally longer;
- eligibility may depend on the individual notice;
- the opportunity may require an established consortium or company;
- finding a notice does not imply realistic executability;
- expected time to first payment is usually inferior to direct
  bounties, freelance work and paid digital challenges.

TED may be revisited later as an exceptional-contract discovery source.

## Ranking rule

Direct executability must be evaluated before nominal reward.

A smaller opportunity with high execution and payment probability must
rank above a large theoretical contract that is unlikely to be won.

Recommended priority formula:

direct_revenue_priority =
    payment_probability
    * execution_probability
    * eligibility_probability
    * delivery_fit
    * reward
    / estimated_total_effort

## Safety

No application, proposal, bid, claim or external submission may occur
without human approval.
