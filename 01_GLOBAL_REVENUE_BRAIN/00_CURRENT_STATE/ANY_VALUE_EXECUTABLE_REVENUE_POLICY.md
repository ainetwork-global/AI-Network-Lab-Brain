# Any-Value Executable Revenue Policy

Updated: 2026-07-17

## Official objective

The Global Revenue Brain may accept opportunities of any monetary value,
including cents, provided that:

- the task is legal;
- the deliverable is clear;
- execution is realistically possible;
- the payment mechanism is verifiable;
- the user can receive the payment;
- the platform or payer has sufficient trust;
- the opportunity does not require prohibited automation;
- no external application or submission occurs without human approval.

## Minimum reward

There is no minimum reward.

A task paying USD 0.10 may be preferable to a theoretical USD 10,000
opportunity when the smaller task has:

- higher execution probability;
- higher payment probability;
- lower effort;
- faster payment;
- fewer eligibility barriers;
- lower competition;
- repeatability;
- evidence that payment is actually made.

## Primary ranking objective

Maximize the probability of receiving real revenue.

The first successful payment is strategically important because it
validates the complete cycle:

discovery
-> eligibility
-> execution
-> delivery
-> approval
-> payment
-> receipt confirmation
-> learning

## Preferred opportunity profile

- remotely executable;
- digital deliverable;
- clear payment amount or payment rule;
- low or zero entry cost;
- accessible from Brazil;
- payment through bank transfer, PayPal, Wise, Payoneer, Stripe,
  stablecoin, crypto wallet or another supported method;
- possible with ChatGPT Plus, Gemini, PowerShell, Python and browser;
- short execution time;
- low qualification burden;
- low legal complexity;
- repeatable when possible.

## Ranking principle

Do not rank only by nominal reward.

Use:

expected_receivable_value =
    reward
    * payment_probability
    * execution_probability
    * eligibility_probability
    * receipt_probability

Then calculate:

revenue_velocity =
    expected_receivable_value
    / estimated_hours_until_payment

Small tasks with high revenue velocity may rank above large but unlikely
opportunities.

## Human approval

Human approval is mandatory before:

- account creation;
- identity verification;
- application;
- proposal;
- bid;
- claim;
- external message;
- task acceptance;
- deliverable submission;
- wallet connection;
- financial transaction.

## Safety

The Brain must not:

- bypass platform rules;
- fake identity or qualifications;
- create deceptive work;
- submit spam;
- automate prohibited interactions;
- perform unauthorized security testing;
- spend money without approval.
