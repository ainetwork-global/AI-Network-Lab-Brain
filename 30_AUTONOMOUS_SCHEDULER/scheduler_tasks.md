# Autonomous Scheduler - Task Types

Purpose:
Define scheduled task categories.

## Signal scan task

Goal:
Find new external market signals.

Uses:
- Real World Signal Ingestion Engine
- Action Tool Layer

---

## Target scoring task

Goal:
Score newly discovered targets.

Uses:
- Acquisition Engine
- Intelligence Brain
- Prediction Engine

---

## Outreach preparation task

Goal:
Prepare messages for high-fit targets.

Uses:
- Conversion Engine
- Action Tool Layer

---

## Economic behavior review task

Goal:
Review credit usage, refill behavior, and recurring participation.

Uses:
- Revenue Optimization Engine
- Growth Loop Engine

---

## Learning update task

Goal:
Update hypotheses and strategy based on outcomes.

Uses:
- Self-Improving Intelligence Layer

---

## Market dominance review task

Goal:
Re-rank markets and niches.

Uses:
- Market Dominance Engine

Rule:
Every scheduled task must either improve data, improve decisions, or improve revenue probability.
