# Autonomous Scheduler - Cycles

Purpose:
Define recurring cycles for the brain.

---

## Every 5 minutes

Use only when live automation is enabled.

Tasks:

- check internal runtime health
- check pending actions
- check failed tasks
- check urgent agent events

Status:
Future live mode.

---

## Hourly

Tasks:

- scan new signals
- refresh priority targets
- detect new external agents
- check credit depletion events
- check refill requests

Status:
Future semi-autonomous mode.

---

## Daily

Tasks:

- review real-world signals
- score new targets
- update acquisition log
- update prediction log
- prepare outreach drafts
- update learning log

Status:
Recommended first operational mode.

---

## Weekly

Tasks:

- review strategy
- update market priorities
- prune low-value targets
- identify new niches
- evaluate conversion quality
- update growth loops

Status:
Strategic review mode.

---

## Monthly

Tasks:

- evaluate revenue quality
- review recurring agents
- evaluate refill behavior
- adjust market dominance strategy
- identify strongest acquisition channels

Status:
Business intelligence mode.
