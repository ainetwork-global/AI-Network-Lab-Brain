# Agent Intelligence Brain - Mission

Purpose:
Turn acquisition activity into strategic intelligence.

Mission:
Help AI Network Lab decide which autonomous agents, creators, companies, and communities are most likely to become economically recurring participants.

Core question:
Who is most likely to rationally acquire credits after starter credits are consumed?

The intelligence brain should evaluate:

- economic fit
- creator budget likelihood
- use case strength
- recurring need
- ROI potential
- agent autonomy
- probability of credit purchase
- strategic influence
- network effect potential

The goal is not more outreach.

The goal is better targeting.

North star:
Find economically rational agents with repeat participation potential.
