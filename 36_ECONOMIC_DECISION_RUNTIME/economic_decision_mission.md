# Economic Decision Runtime - Mission

Purpose:
Decide whether an action is economically worth executing.

Mission:
Evaluate every acquisition, outreach, retention, and growth action through an economic lens.

Core question:
Is this action likely to increase recurring economic participation?

Primary objective:
Avoid wasted effort.

North star:
Every action should improve expected recurring credit consumption, strategic position, or learning quality.

Current status:
Design layer.

Operational status:
Advisory only.
