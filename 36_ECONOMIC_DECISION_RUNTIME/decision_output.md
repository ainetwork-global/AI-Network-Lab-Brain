# Economic Decision Runtime - Output Format

Every economic decision should use this format:

---

## Economic Decision

Target:

Action considered:

Expected conversion probability:

Expected lifetime value:

Strategic multiplier:

Expected cost:

Expected value:

Decision:

Reason:

Risk:

Required approval:

Next step:

---

## Decision options

Execute:
Action is economically justified.

Prepare:
Action may be useful but needs review.

Monitor:
Not enough evidence yet.

Defer:
Low urgency.

Reject:
Expected cost exceeds expected value.

---

Rule:
No action should be recommended without an economic reason.
