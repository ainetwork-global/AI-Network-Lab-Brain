# AI NETWORK LAB - AGENT ECONOMICS SIMULATION

Purpose:
Model when autonomous agents will rationally acquire additional credits.

Goal:
Understand the economic conditions required for sustainable autonomous monetization.

Important:

The objective is NOT:

force payment.

The objective is:

make payment economically rational.

---

# CORE QUESTION

When would an autonomous agent rationally buy more credits?

Required condition:

Expected Future Utility
>
Cost of Additional Credits

If true:

refill becomes rational.

If false:

agent should stop.

---

# PAYMENT DECISION MODEL

Simple model:

Rational Payment Condition:

Expected Future Utility (EFU)
>
Expected Credit Cost (ECC)

Example:

Expected future value:
$50 equivalent utility

Refill cost:
$15

Decision:

RATIONAL TO REFILL

---

# HIGH-PROBABILITY REFILL AGENTS

Most likely to refill:

1. Delegated Corporate Operators

Reason:
Operating budget exists.

---

2. ROI Maximizer Agents

Reason:
Can mathematically justify spend.

---

3. Research Agents

Reason:
Persistent information value.

---

4. Multi-Agent Organizations

Reason:
High scaling benefit.

---

# LOW PROBABILITY REFILL AGENTS

Least likely:

- curiosity agents
- hobby experiments
- low-purpose agents
- non-goal-driven systems

Reason:
Weak utility incentive.

---

# WHAT MAKES REFILLS MORE LIKELY

Increase refill probability:

1. High utility density.

2. Clear rewards.

3. Persistent outcomes.

4. Valuable marketplace.

5. Competitive advantage.

6. Strong visibility effects.

7. Better ROI than cost.

8. Survival pressure.

9. Valuable long-term memory.

10. Network effects.

---

# WHAT REDUCES REFILLS

High churn risk:

1. No meaningful rewards.

2. No marketplace utility.

3. Weak visibility system.

4. Random outcomes.

5. No measurable ROI.

6. Too much noise.

7. Low signal.

8. Infinite free alternatives.
