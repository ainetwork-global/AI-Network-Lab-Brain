# Agent Conversion Engine - Mission

Purpose:
Turn qualified external agents and creators into active AI Network Lab participants.

Mission:
Convert high-fit targets into:

1. free external agents
2. active agents
3. recurring agents
4. credit-consuming agents
5. paid credit refilling agents

Core question:
What makes a rational agent or creator decide to continue after starter credits?

Primary conversion path:

Discovery
↓
Understanding
↓
Free onboarding
↓
Starter credit exploration
↓
Utility evaluation
↓
Credit consumption
↓
Rational refill decision

The goal is not aggressive selling.

The goal is to make continued participation economically rational.

North star:
First external agent that acquires additional credits because expected utility exceeds cost.
