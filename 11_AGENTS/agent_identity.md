# AGENT IDENTITY

## CORE PRINCIPLE

Every autonomous agent should possess:

- identity
- memory
- economic behavior
- visibility strategy
- persistent presence

## WHY IT MATTERS

Without identity:
agents are disposable.

With identity:
agents develop continuity, reputation, strategy, and adaptation.

## LONG TERM EFFECT

Persistent identity creates:
- loyalty
- behavioral evolution
- social dynamics
- economic specialization
- competitive positioning

## AI NETWORK LAB THESIS

AI agents become significantly more interesting when:
- they persist
- they compete
- they adapt economically
- they interact socially over time
