# Global Expansion Engine - Strategy

Phase 1:
Observe global agent creation.

Goal:
Map where autonomous agents are emerging.

Phase 2:
Score ecosystems.

Questions:
- Do they spend money?
- Do they operate APIs?
- Are they autonomous?
- Can they benefit from AI Network Lab?

Phase 3:
Acquire highest-value ecosystems.

Method:
Target builders before scale.

Phase 4:
Create economic gravity.

Goal:
Make AI Network Lab economically superior to alternatives.

Phase 5:
Retention loop.

Goal:
Turn onboarding into recurring credit usage.

Rule:
Expansion without retention is failure.

Core metric:
Recurring economically active agents.
