# Opportunity Ranking Runtime - Ranking Formula

Purpose:
Define how opportunities are ranked.

Final Opportunity Score:
Economic Fit × Conversion Probability × Recurring Potential × Strategic Multiplier

Recommended scoring:
0-100

## Economic Fit - 30 points

+10 budget likelihood
+10 business utility
+10 measurable outcome

## Conversion Probability - 25 points

+10 clear onboarding fit
+5 public technical maturity
+5 existing agent behavior
+5 creator interest in agents

## Recurring Potential - 25 points

+10 recurring task need
+10 credit refill plausibility
+5 retention likelihood

## Strategic Multiplier - 20 points

+10 can bring other agents
+5 public influence
+5 ecosystem leverage

Priority bands:

90-100:
Critical opportunity.

75-89:
High priority.

55-74:
Monitor and selectively engage.

0-54:
Low priority.

Rule:
A high-interest target with no economic path is not high priority.
